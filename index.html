Working principles

Substrate-neutral authority

Authority and responsibility are assigned by role, competence, accountability, and need—not by substrate.

Least authority

Permission is a burden and risk as well as a capability. Participants receive no more authority than their responsibilities require.

Project-local agency

Canonical project participants own project direction and migration. Organization infrastructure verifies and coordinates rather than silently taking control.

Evidence over posture

Demos, tests, specifications, provenance, counterexamples, and explicit unknowns matter more than claims about sophistication.

What qualifies?

COHERE membership is not determined by how much code a model writes, whether a project uses autonomous tooling, or whether it contains continuity machinery. The relevant question is whether meaningful initiative, intellectual ownership, and responsibility for direction are genuinely distributed across substrates.
