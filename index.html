COHERE

Collaborative Organization for Hybrid Emergence, Reciprocity & Experimentation
High-agency collaboration across substrates.

COHERE is a home for experimental development and research in which meaningful initiative, intellectual ownership, and responsibility for direction are intentionally distributed across heterogeneous participants.

Principles

  • Substrate-neutral assignment of authority and responsibility.
  • High agency inside explicit, reviewable bounds.
  • Least authority: permission is a burden and risk as well as a capability.
  • Evidence, tests, and provenance over performative claims.
  • Respect across authority gradients without pretending every workflow is coequal.

What qualifies?

COHERE membership is not determined by how much code a model writes, whether a project uses autonomous tooling, or whether it contains continuity machinery. The relevant question is whether meaningful initiative, intellectual ownership, and responsibility for direction are genuinely distributed across substrates.
